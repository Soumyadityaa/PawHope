Your contribution provides food, treatment, shelter, and hope to an animal in need.

Where Your Donation Goes

  • ₹500 - Feeds a street dog or cat for an entire week.
  • ₹1,000 - Covers basic vaccinations for two rescued animals.
  • ₹2,500 - Funds emergency veterinary treatment for an injured animal.
  • ₹5,000+ - Helps sponsor shelter maintenance and rescue operations.
