From a Broken Leg to Running Freely

Animal: Max (Dog) Rescued: May 15, 2026 Result: Adopted

Max was found on the side of a busy highway, unable to walk due to a severe fracture in his hind leg. Upon receiving the emergency call, our ambulance arrived within 20 minutes.

He underwent a complex orthopedic surgery funded entirely by community donations. After two months of physical rehabilitation at our shelter, Max regained full mobility. Today, he lives with a loving family and enjoys long daily runs in the park.

Trapped in a Pipeline: Luna's Rescue

Animal: Luna (Cat) Rescued: July 20, 2026 Result: Foster Care

Little Luna, an 8-month-old kitten, got stuck inside an abandoned drainage pipe during a heavy rainstorm. A local resident heard her cries and contacted our helpline.

Our team worked for three hours to safely extract her without causing injury. She was shivering, malnourished, and terrified. With warm blankets, nutritious meals, and endless patience from our volunteers, Luna blossomed into a highly affectionate cat.
