Our Mission

Dedicated to improving the lives of animals living in urban areas through rapid response and sustainable care.

Rescue

Respond to animals suffering from accidents, injuries, abandonment, or unsafe urban environments.

Food & Water

Provide nutritious food and clean drinking water to animals in need across the city.

Medical Care

Help injured and sick animals receive prompt and professional veterinary treatment.

Shelter

Provide safe temporary or long-term shelter for vulnerable and recovering animals.
